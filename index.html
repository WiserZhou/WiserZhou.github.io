Yufan Zhou 周雨凡

Ph.D. Student at HKU CDS, advised by Prof. Difan Zou.

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing and Data Science (CDS) at The University of Hong Kong (HKU), where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Difan Zou. Prior to joining HKU, I received my B.Eng. degree in Software Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT) (Rank 2/231, National Scholarship recipient), advised by Prof. Weigang Zhang and Prof. Shuhui Wang.

My research centers on Embodied AI and 3D Vision & Generation. I aim to build spatially grounded, physically plausible 3D world representations and generalist embodied agents capable of perceiving, reasoning, and interacting with complex physical environments. Previously, I was a research intern at MMLab@NTU (with Prof. Xingang Pan), MMLab@HKU & VAST (with Prof. Xihui Liu and Yan-Pei Cao), and Westlake University (with Prof. Huan Wang).

3D Generation Part-Aware Modeling Rectified Flow

OmniPart: Part-Aware 3D Generation with Semantic Decoupling and Structural Cohesion

Yunhan Yang*, Yufan Zhou*, Yuan-Chen Guo, Zi-Xin Zou, Yukun Huang, Ying-Tian Liu, Hao Xu, Ding Liang, Yan-Pei Cao✉, Xihui Liu✉

A two-stage framework for editable, part-aware 3D object generation that couples an autoregressive 3D part bounding-box structure planner with a spatially-conditioned rectified flow generator—achieving high semantic decoupling and geometric cohesion across articulated and multi-part 3D assets.

Masked Temporal Interpolation Diffusion for Procedure Planning in Instructional Videos

Yufan Zhou, Zhaobo Qi, Lingshuai Lin, Junqi Jing, Tingting Chai, Beichen Zhang, Shuhui Wang, Weigang Zhang

Addresses goal-directed procedure planning from visual start and goal observations by introducing a latent-space temporal interpolation module and a task-adaptive masked proximity loss, enabling coherent intermediate state reasoning and state-of-the-art action sequence prediction.
